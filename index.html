The Blessing Remains

The Power of Durva

Analasura's destructive fire was overcome, and the humble Durva grass brought relief to Ganesha. From that sacred memory grew a cherished offering: 21 blades of Durva offered with devotion to Lord Ganesha.
Story Note: The Analasura legend is preserved in regional Puranic and devotional traditions, with variations in details across retellings. This comic presents the devotional retelling used for this chapter and its association with the Durva offering.
